REMORFIN Android Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026.

Float Dev develops REMORFIN Android. For privacy questions or requests, contact floatdev1@gmail.com.

Information used by the game

This Android edition stores game progress, records and settings locally on your device. It does not require an account. The current edition does not send these saved records to Float Dev and does not include advertising, in-app purchases, cloud saves, or developer analytics and crash-reporting services.

The feedback questionnaire used in some desktop versions is disabled in this Android edition. Opening or playing the game does not submit that questionnaire or its response identifier to Google Forms.

Local saves and device controls

You can remove locally stored game data through Android's app storage controls. Doing so removes your local progress and settings. Android or your device provider may offer backup and restore under your device settings and their own policies.

Support and voluntary feedback

If you email Float Dev, we receive your email address and anything you choose to include. We use that information to reply and investigate your report. Please avoid sending passwords, payment details or other sensitive information. Support correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle your request and applicable obligations. You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion, subject to applicable requirements.

Google Play and other services

Google Play distributes the game and may process download, testing, review and diagnostic information through its own services. If you send test feedback through Google Play, Google processes that submission and makes relevant feedback available to the developer. Your use of Google's services is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising in this edition.

Children

REMORFIN contains violence and mature themes and is not intended for children. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children. Contact us if you believe a child has sent us personal information so that we can address it.

Your choices and questions

Contact Float Dev at the email address above about information you have sent us. Depending on your location, you may have additional privacy rights, including the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

Changes

We will update this policy when the Android edition's data practices change. The date on this page identifies the current policy text.
